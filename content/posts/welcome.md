Welcome to Prayer Book Notes, a space for reflecting on the Daily Office from the 1979 Book of Common Prayer.

## Purpose

This blog will explore the daily readings, collects, and prayers appointed in the Episcopal Church's prayer book, offering commentary and personal reflections.

## What to Expect

Regular posts will include:
- Scripture reflections from the Daily Office lectionary
- Commentary on collects and prayers
- Observations on the liturgical seasons
- Thoughts on spiritual practice

May these notes serve as a companion to your own engagement with the Daily Office.
