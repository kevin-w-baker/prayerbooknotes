Prayer Book Notes is a personal reflection on the Daily Office from the Episcopal Church's 1979 Book of Common Prayer.

Each post explores the appointed readings, prayers, and collects from Morning and Evening Prayer, offering commentary and personal reflections on these timeless liturgical texts.

## About the Book of Common Prayer

The 1979 Book of Common Prayer is the official prayer book of the Episcopal Church in the United States. It contains orders for worship, prayers, and the Daily Office of Morning and Evening Prayer.
